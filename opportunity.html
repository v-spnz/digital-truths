Opportunities

Introduction

There are no positive opportunities for AI in using Misinformation and Disinformation. The opportunities of AI in the use of Misinformation and Disinformation are all negative, these opportunities include easier creation of misinformation using LLM such as chat GPT and the likes, The abuse of AI algorithms on social media, and the easier creation of Deepfakes.

LLM and AI

Large language models such as chat GPT can be used to create both misinformation and disinformation at a speed faster than humans can produce it. This is a negative opportunity as it gives people the opportunity to produce more misinformation and disinformation, making it so that they can spread it faster and push more out. As LLM gets better this opportunity is going to become worse as LLM will be able to produce more and better misinformation and disinformation.

AI algorithms

The use of AI algorithms on social media is a negative for the spread of misinformation and disinformation. Even though AI algorithms can have benefits on social media such as showing you stuff you are interested in on your feed it can be easily abused to spread misinformation and disinformation to a large audience and reinforce this information in people's minds. People can abuse these algorithms by posting misinformation and disinformation and using the algorithm to spread it to people social media feeds. This is a negative opportunity of AI in the spread of misinformation and disinformation as they use the AI to spread it to people.

Deepfakes

A negative opportunity of the use of AI in spreading misinformation and disinformation is in the creation of deepfakes and progress of deepfakes becoming better and harder to distinguish from real videos. As AI improves the creation of deepfakes becomes both better and faster, this means that deepfakes can be created at a faster rate and harder for people to tell from real videos. This helps misinformation and disinformation as it means that videos can be created of celebrities and politicians saying things that they haven’t said or endorsing products or ideas that they do not make it harder for people to tell from real videos and endorsements.

Conclusion

In conclusion we can see that there are many negative opportunities from Ai in the spread of misinformation and disinformation but there are no positive opportunities. This shows how the use of AI for misinformation and disinformation is dangerous and should be both regulated by governments and controlled by Ai companies.
