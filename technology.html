Technology / Topic

Why We Chose This Topic

The improvement of artificial intelligence has significantly increased within the last two years. This improvement of AI can be seen in the quality and realism of AI-generated content from companies such as Sora AI, making it harder to distinguish between real and fake media.

Given the rapid advancements in AI and the increasing sophistication of fake media, there is a pressing need for greater public awareness and education. Many people are still unaware of the existence and capabilities of deepfake technology, making them vulnerable to manipulation. Educating the public about the potential risks and how to critically evaluate digital content is crucial in mitigating the impact of disinformation. By raising awareness, we can empower individuals to recognize and question the authenticity of the media they consume, fostering a more informed and resilient society.

Real-World Impact of Disinformation

Documented cases show how deepfakes have been used to mislead the public and disrupt political processes. A notable example is the case of Manoj Tiwari during the 2020 Legislative Assembly elections in Delhi. In this instance, two deepfake videos of Tiwari, a leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), were created using AI to lip-sync new audio tracks in English and Haryanvi, languages he does not speak. These videos were widely circulated, reaching approximately 15 million people through WhatsApp groups. This manipulation of digital content to mislead voters highlights the growing challenge of combating disinformation in the digital age, where advanced AI technologies can easily produce highly convincing fake media (Langguth et al., 2021).

Another example is the use of deepfake technology by Extinction Rebellion Belgium to create a video of Belgian premier Sophie Wilmès. The video altered a previous address by Wilmès to depict the COVID-19 pandemic as a consequence of environmental destruction, illustrating how deepfakes can be used to manipulate political messages and public opinion (Langguth et al., 2021). These cases demonstrate the real-world impact of AI-generated fake media on society and the urgency of addressing this issue.

Conclusion

Our decision to focus on the topic of fake media and AI is driven by the rapid technological advancements, the ethical and societal implications, the real-world impact of disinformation, and the need for greater public awareness and education. Understanding the capabilities and potential misuse of AI in creating fake media is essential in addressing the challenges posed by disinformation in the digital age. Through our website, we aim to shed light on these issues and contribute to the ongoing efforts to combat the spread of false information.
